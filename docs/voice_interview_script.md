# Example Voice Interview Script

**Role:** Senior Backend Engineer

1. Greeting
   - "Hi {{candidate_name}}, welcome to your interview for the {{job_role}} role."
2. Behavioral
   - "Tell me about a time you resolved a production incident. What was your approach?"
3. Technical
   - "Walk me through how you would design a low-latency audio streaming pipeline."
4. Follow-up
   - "What tradeoffs would you make to ensure reliability under heavy load?"
5. Closing
   - "Thanks for sharing your experience. Do you have any questions for us?"
